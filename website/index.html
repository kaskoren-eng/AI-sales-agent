QUESTIONS. ANSWERED.
Questions

Straight answers.

Does it sound like a robot?
No. It speaks Hebrew that sounds human, not flat narration and not a rigid script. It changes pace with the conversation, handles objections and responds to what was actually said. If a lead asks directly, the agent says honestly that it is an AI agent, and that transparency turns out to build trust rather than cost you the call.
What happens when a lead asks something it doesn't know?
It recognises the edge of what it knows and hands over cleanly to a person. It does not invent answers. It says it will have the right person get back to them, and steers to booking a meeting where that can happen.
How long does setup take?
Up to five business days from signing. The first onboarding call takes about an hour. We learn your business, your ICP and the objections you hear most. Then we build it, test it with you, and put it live. There is no setup fee and no implementation project.
Will it connect to our CRM?
monday.com, Airtable and Google Sheets are connected today, with Fireberry in build. Everything else goes through the REST API and webhooks, which covers Make, Zapier and n8n. If you're on something specific, bring it to the demo and we'll confirm compatibility there rather than guess.
Which languages does it work in?
Hebrew first. That is what the whole voice stack was built for, and it is the reason it sounds the way it does. It switches to English when a lead does. The dashboard you manage it from is in English, with Hebrew available; the language your agent speaks to leads is a separate setting.
What about privacy and our data?
Your lead data is yours. It is stored on secured servers, never sold on, and never used to train models. We work to the Israeli Privacy Protection Law and GDPR. Recording notice, opt-out and quiet hours are enforced by the system itself, not left to configuration.
