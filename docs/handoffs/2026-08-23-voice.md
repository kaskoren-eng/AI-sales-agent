## The new problem: the rolling slot loses a fragmented question

She was asked whether the agent works on Shabbat and evenings and answered *"אין לי כרגע את המידע
הזה"*. **The KB has that answer** — a direct probe scores the availability chunk **0.576** on her own
garbled transcript and **0.688** on the clean phrasing. Retrieval was never the problem.

What happened is that STT split the question across four turns:

```
138s  "מה? איך."                                    -> too_short, then a slot that EXPIRED empty
145s  "זה עובד, הסוכן? ... הוא עובד גם בשבת, ב—"    -> slot, 3 chunks
146s  "בשעות לילה, בערב. איך זה עובד?"              -> slot, 3 chunks
148s  "בהקשר הזה."                                  -> slot, 3 chunks (for a meaningless tail)
163s  she answers, un-grounded
```

The slot is rebuilt from the **last committed user turn** and lives for exactly one inference. When
the question arrives in pieces, the fragment that reaches the final inference is a tail like
"בהקשר הזה" — and the slot that actually held the answer was discarded two turns earlier.

**This is a cost of R2.1 specifically.** Under R2's cumulative injection those chunks were still in
context when she finally spoke. The context diet traded that away. Same mechanism at 65s: fragments
at 63/64/64, then an expired empty slot at 65s, then a 61-word un-grounded answer.

This call had **6 fragmented turns of 14**. It is now the top RAG issue, ahead of threshold tuning.

Candidate directions, none built: build the slot from the last N user turns rather than the last one;
or carry the previous slot forward when the current turn is a fragment; or merge fragments before
they reach `llmNode`.

## The <1s target is not reachable on this stack

Measured chain, gpt-5.4, synthetic audio:

```
EOU        226ms   (real calls: 477-747ms — Hebrew has no end-of-turn model)
TTFT     1,048ms
TTS TTFB   485ms
        ────────
         1,759ms   (~2,000ms on a real call)
```

To land under 1,000ms, TTFT + TTS would have to total ~300ms combined. No provider in the bench does
that with Hebrew worth shipping: the two fastest (grok 583ms, gemini-flash-lite 537ms) "won" by
answering `שאלה טובה. עם מי אני מדברת?` — fast because they said nothing.

Honest floor today: **~1.5s synthetic, ~1.8-2.0s on a real line.** Getting below that needs a faster
Hebrew TTS or an end-of-turn model that speaks Hebrew, not another prompt or cache tweak.

⚠️ `VOICE_TURN_DETECTION=stt` is NOT the answer and must not be tried again — it was tested on a real
call, cut the caller off ten times and went silent three times, while posting the best-ever
endpointing median (259ms). The metric was a trap. See `phase-4-known-issues.md` §11 and the comment
in `env.ts`.
