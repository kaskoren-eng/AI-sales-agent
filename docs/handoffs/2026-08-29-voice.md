## The finding that reframes P1-1 and P1-3

The worklist attributed the "word … long silence … sentence" pattern to the thinking filler. It is
not the filler. Both offending words — `"אהה."` @29.3s and `"כן."` @186.8s — are members of
`ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS_HE`, the instant-ack bank. So are the two standalone `"אהה."` turns in P1-3.

Mechanism, read out of the SDK rather than inferred from timings
(`node_modules/@livekit/agents/dist/voice/agent_activity.js`, `produceSegments`):

> `startSegment()` — and therefore `ttsNode` — runs on the **first text chunk of an inference
> step**. `llmNode` injects the acknowledgement before the model has written anything. So a step
> whose only real output is a **tool call** still speaks one word. The tool then runs
> (`capture_lead_info` 1025ms, `request_human_handoff` 971ms), a **second** inference step starts,
> and that step injects its **own** acknowledgement in front of the real sentence.

One caller turn → two receipts with a multi-second hole between them. That is the "script" Koren
heard, and it is ours, not the model's.

## What changed

### P1-1 — the filler / opener (commit `31a6fd4`)

1. **One acknowledgement per reply, not per inference step** — new `turn-opener.ts`
   (`chooseTurnOpener`, `chunkCallsTool`). A step that follows a tool call opens with a
   **hesitation** instead of a second receipt, or with **nothing** when the call has spent its
   filler budget. The tool call is detected from `delta.toolCalls`, the same field
   `performLLMInference` itself reads.
2. **`withFiller` holds the hesitation until the model's real words are in hand**, and drops it if
   they never come (`speech-guard.ts`). The acknowledgement is passed through untouched and unheld —
   that ordering is the whole <1s feature. A filler can now only be followed by the TTS chunking
   delay, structurally.
3. **No filler word repeats within a call** — `ThinkingFillerLedger` (`thinking-fillers.he.ts`).
   `offer()` costs nothing; only `commit()` spends the budget, so a filler armed onto a tool-call
   step (and therefore never spoken) no longer burns one of the call's three.

### P1-2 — the spoken register (commit `ac8a0b6`)

Root cause found: **the register section was fighting the Speech Rhythm section and losing.** With
`VOICE_INSTANT_ACK` on, Speech Rhythm forbids opening a reply with a reaction word — and every
example the register gave was exactly such an opener (`"סבבה, אז נתקדם."`). Told to use a device and
shown only forbidden demonstrations of it, the model dropped the device. Round 5 (17/17) scored TTS
samples, never a live LLM turn, which is why nothing caught it.

The section is now a **quota** ("roughly every second or third reply"), carries a **placement rule
that follows the instant-ack flag** (ack on → inside the sentence, never first; ack off → her own
short opener is the natural home), uses **only non-opener examples** (a test fails if that
regresses), and ends with a self-check ("if it would look normal in a formal email…").

**No new Hebrew entered the slang bank.** `SPOKEN_REGISTER_SLANG` is unchanged — every member passed
round-5 screening (synth → 8kHz phone band → Soniox round-trip) and nothing joins it without that
gate. The new examples reuse only words already in it.

### P1-3 — the two transcript bugs

1. `"כן."` answering `"מה המצב, קרן?"` — **removed from `ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS_HE`** (commit `264ec60`).
   Every remaining member survives landing on a question; "כן." means *yes* and the caller cannot
   know we were not answering him. The Speech Rhythm section quoted the same list and is corrected;
   a test now pins the two together. Three words left — enough for the never-twice-running rule.
2. Standalone `"אהה."` turns — same root cause as P1-1. **Half fixed:** the duplicate second receipt
   is gone and the hesitation now lands in front of real words. The acknowledgement on the
   tool-calling step is still a standalone utterance. See "What is NOT fixed" below.

### P2 — the handoff summary (commit `6e65412`)

She now asks **ONE** short question — `"רק שאדע להעביר — על מה תרצה לדבר איתו?"` — and the owner
alert carries the four things a person needs before dialling back: who called, what they want to
discuss, what is already established, why they asked for a human.

**The invariant, stated in the prompt, repeated in the tool description, and pinned by a test that
hands off with `wants=null, context=null`: a handoff is never blocked, delayed or downgraded because
the caller will not explain themselves.** One question; the tool fires either way.

The summary is built from both sides so either half can be missing: `wants` / `context` from the
model, `established` from `rt.callState.facts` (the working memory `capture_lead_info` already
fills). A lead who says nothing still arrives with his business, budget and timeline attached; a
call with the state machine off still sends an alert.

New fields are `.nullable().optional()`, not `.optional()` — gpt-5.4 sends explicit `null` and a
bare `.optional()` rejects it (the bug that cost `capture_lead_info` 20–44s silences). Still no
destination parameter; every field is length-capped. The WhatsApp template keeps its four approved
slots, with the summary folded onto one line inside the reason slot.

### CI fix (same commit, requested mid-task)

`request-human-handoff.tool.test.ts`'s fake db did `Object.assign(where(), …)`, which **invoked**
`where()` and built a rejected promise on the `failWrites` path whether or not the code awaited it —
and `.where().returning()` never does. Every test passed and the run still exited 1. It is a lazy
thenable now: the rejection exists only when somebody awaits. Intent unchanged (a dead DB must not
block a handoff).

## Kill-switches

| Switch | Default | What it now also controls |
|---|---|---|
| `VOICE_THINKING_FILLER_MS` | `2500` | `0` disables the 2.5s think-timer **and** the post-tool hesitation opener (a post-tool step then opens with nothing — never a second receipt). |
| `VOICE_INSTANT_ACK` | `true` | Off = nothing is injected at all, so the whole turn-opener mechanism is inert. That config cannot produce an orphaned word: with no injection a tool step yields no text chunk, no segment, no speech. |
| `VOICE_SPOKEN_REGISTER_ENABLED` | `true` | Unchanged — off drops the whole register section, including the new placement paragraph. |

No new env var was added.

## What a human must verify on a real call

1. **The gap after an acknowledgement.** Expect: one receipt, silence, then `"אממ... <sentence>"` —
   never receipt, silence, receipt, sentence. Grep the agent log for `thinking_filler` and check for
   `"reason":"after_tool_call"`.
2. **No repeated filler word** in one call, and never more than three (`n` / `max` in the same log
   line, and `spoken:true` now marks the ones actually heard).
3. **Slang, in at least two turns**, inside sentences and never as the first word. This is the one
   change that cannot be verified by test — it is model behaviour under a rewritten prompt.
4. **The handoff.** Ask for a human, then *refuse to say why* — she must hand off anyway. Then do it
   again and answer the question; the owner alert should carry the answer plus the business facts.
   ⚠️ This still depends on P0-1 (migration 0017) and P0-2 (`tenants.settings.handoff` for
   ClickScales); until those land the alert has nowhere to go and the DB flag still fails.
5. **Latency numbers as usual** — EOU / LLM TTFT / TTS first byte / worst case / dead air. Nothing
   here should move the medians: no stage was added to the path, and the acknowledgement still
   leaves first and unheld.

## What is NOT fixed, and what I decided against

- **The acknowledgement on a tool-calling step is still followed by ~2–2.5s of silence** (the tool
  round-trip plus the next step's TTFT) before she speaks again. It is no longer a *word–gap–word*
  stutter, but it is not under the worklist's ~1.5s bar either. **The remaining lever is the tool,
  not the speech path:** `capture_lead_info` at 1025ms is roughly 40% of that gap and is a pure
  bookkeeping write the model never reads. Making it non-blocking would very likely bring the gap
  under the bar. I did **not** do it here — it changes when `rt.leadId` is resolved for a later
  `book_meeting`, and it would hide a genuine write failure from the model. It deserves its own
  change, its own kill-switch and its own tests, not a rider on this one.
- **Suppressing the acknowledgement on tool steps is impossible with the ack kept instant.** The
  only signal that a step is a tool call is `delta.toolCalls`, which arrives at TTFT (~1.1s) — by
  then the word is already committed to audio. Waiting for it would cost the entire <1s mechanism on
  *every* turn to fix ~half of them. Not a trade I would make without Koren.
- **Our injected words land in the model's own chat history.** `performLLMInference` folds every
  chunk, ours included, into `data.generatedText` — so on a tool step her committed turn is
  literally `"אהה."`, and the model sees itself producing standalone receipt-turns. That may be part
  of why it started emitting them on its own. Fixing it means editing the committed item at a turn
  boundary (where `trimHistory` already works safely) — worth a look, out of scope today.
- **I did not raise `VOICE_THINKING_FILLER_MS`.** It was one of the worklist's options, but it tunes
  *when* she hesitates, and the bug was *where*. The structural fix makes the timing much less
  load-bearing; the knob is still there if a live call says otherwise.
